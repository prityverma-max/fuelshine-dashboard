      Moat & IP protection

      Scoped this sprint from the USP/moat/R&D skill — not legal advice; confirm every filing decision with an IP/patent attorney before spending against it.

      💡
      The moat isn't the tracking. Phone GPS and Smartcar OEM access are commoditized — a funded competitor gets both on day one. The defensible pieces are the compounding verified-trip dataset, the shared cross-team trust in one record, the flywheel, and the not-yet-built risk-scoring model. Protect those, not the capture method.
      ⚠
      Two of the three items asked about this round don't belong on a patent-scoping list. Employee mileage reimbursement (GPS capture + IRS/CRA compliance mapping) sits in a crowded prior-art field — MileIQ, Everlance, TripLog, Motus and Cardata all do GPS trip capture, and complying with a public IRS/CRA rule isn't itself inventive. Company policy & compliance gating is worse than crowded — Prolius already ships exactly this, live, today. That's direct prior art, not a hypothetical competitor risk. Fuel Receipt Verification AI is the one of the three that actually belongs in the scoping call — see row 4.
